# truck-cabin — exact prompts

## Plate v2 (accepted, 2026-11-05, Google Vids > Image panel, Nano Banana)

```
Generate exactly ONE image only, no storyboard, no extra images. First-person POV from
the driver's seat, no people in frame, no hands, portrait 9:16. Place: the inside of a
colourfully decorated Indian truck cabin, a dashboard crowded with a small Ganesh idol,
a marigold garland, a steel chai glass and a tiffin box, a lime-and-chilli charm and red
tassels swinging from the mirror, painted Bollywood-style stickers on the doors, a narrow
sleeper berth behind the seats, a wide windshield showing a highway. The decorations are
painted flowers, patterns and pictures only, with no letters, no words and no brand logos
anywhere. Lighting: soft warm morning light. Style: Pixar-style 3D animation, soft
cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field. No text, no labels, no watermarks.
```

Result: no lettering anywhere; Ganesh idol, marigold garland, chai glass, tiffin, charm
and tassels, steering wheel lower left, sleeper berth right, open highway ahead.

## Plate v1 (rejected)

Same prompt without the "decorations are painted flowers, patterns and pictures only…"
sentence. It came out with painted lettering ("HORN PLEASE", and what looked like a real
truck brand name on the door). That breaks the set's own rule (no brand names, no text) and
Veo copies reference lettering badly, so it was not used.

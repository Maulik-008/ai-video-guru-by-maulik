# The quality bar

Check the finished video against this before handing it over. A no anywhere is either a fix or something to say out loud.

## The first two seconds
- Something is already happening — a question, a promise, or something wrong.
- No logo, no slow drift in, no establishing shot that establishes nothing.
- The subject is on screen and in focus in frame one.

## Rhythm
- Something changes at least every three seconds: a cut, a reveal, a reaction.
- No shot outstays its beat. If 8 seconds felt long, it should have been 6.
- The ending lands on a beat. It does not fade out on a held frame.

## Faces and continuity (the one people notice)
- The same character is recognisably the same person in every shot: face, build, hair, wardrobe, the signature prop.
- No extra limbs, no duplicated faces, no hands with the wrong number of fingers in a close-up.
- Eyelines match across a cut — two people talking look at each other, not past each other.
- The set looks like the same place from shot to shot: same light, same time of day, same weather.

## Framing
- Nothing important is cropped: no head cut at the top, no mouth behind a caption.
- In 9:16, faces sit in the middle third and nothing that matters is in the bottom 15%, where platform UI sits.
- The clip is natively vertical, not a cropped 16:9.

## Sound
- Every line is intelligible and the lip sync holds.
- Loudness is even from shot to shot — no clip noticeably louder than its neighbours.
- No clipped first syllable, no silence where a line should be.
- Music sits under the dialogue, never over it. Ambience changes when the place changes.
- One voice per character, the same in every shot.

## Text
- Any on-screen text was added in Vids, not generated by Veo, and is spelled correctly.
- Captions are readable at phone size, high contrast, inside the safe margins, and on screen while the words are spoken.
- No stray generated text, no watermark artefacts in frame.

## Honesty
- Everything that drifted, failed or was substituted is in the hand-over message.
- No shot is presented as planned when it was regenerated into something different.
- Credits spent are reported, including retries.

## How to review it

Watch it once as a viewer, straight through, without the plan. Then name the single weakest shot and what is wrong with it in one line. One targeted regeneration beats three vague ones, and the budget is finite.

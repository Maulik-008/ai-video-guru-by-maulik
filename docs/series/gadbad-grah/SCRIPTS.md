# Gadbad Grah: Season 1 scripts (v2)

Every line is romanised Hinglish exactly as it should be spoken. The last shot of most reels is **Bhondu's closing question** (the 6-second stinger): an open-ended question to the viewer, never a request to comment a word, tag or share (see PRODUCTION.md for why).
Word budgets per shot: 6 s up to 14 hard (aim for 10), 8 s up to 18 hard (aim for 13). Generated from [episodes.json](episodes.json); edit one and keep the other in step.

**Season totals:** 18 reels, 142 shots, about 1094 seconds of footage. Every reel is under 90 seconds.

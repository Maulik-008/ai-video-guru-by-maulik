## Anchor image (2026-11-05, Google Vids → Image panel, Nano Banana)

```
Generate exactly ONE image only, no storyboard, no extra images. Pixar-style 3D
character render, full body, standing, centred, plain neutral grey background, soft
even studio lighting. Subject: a plump 62-year-old Indian grandmother, Pixar-style 3D,
round warm face with deep smile lines, silver hair in a neat bun, round gold-rimmed
glasses, a small red bindi, wearing a green cotton saree with a yellow border and a
pale blue cardigan. No text, no labels, no watermarks.
```

Result: accepted on the first generation. Silver bun, round glasses, red bindi, green
saree with a yellow border, pale blue cardigan, warm smile. **Minor drift:** she reads
slim rather than "plump". If a later video needs her rounder, change the word in the
frozen description deliberately and regenerate the anchor — do not just add "plump" to a
shot prompt.
